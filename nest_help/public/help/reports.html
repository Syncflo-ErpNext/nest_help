What This Is

The Reports tile opens a short list of the reports set up for you, so you never have to hunt through menus. Each report covers your own store or warehouses. The office decides who sees which report; ask them if you need one that is not on your list.

Stock On Hand

1 Click Reports, then Stock On Hand.
2 If you look after more than one warehouse, pick it in Warehouse at the top. Your choice is remembered next time.
3 Type part of a name or code in the search box, or pick a category (Home, Ceramics or Fashion) to narrow the list. Each line shows the item, its code and category, and how many are on hand.

The two boxes at the top show how many items match and how many units that is. The list shows the first 300 lines; search to find the rest.

Stock Count List

The list to count from: everything on hand in the warehouse now, plus anything that moved in or out in the last 60 days. A piece that sold out since the last count is still on it, so it gets counted as 0. Items that have not been in the store for 60 days are left off.

1 Click Reports, then Stock Count List, and pick the warehouse if you have more than one.
2 To count one category at a time, pick it in the category box. The download and the printout follow that choice.
3 Click Download count sheet for an Excel sheet, or Print count sheet for paper. Write or type the counted quantity next to each item.
The Excel sheet starts with the columns item_code and qty, the same as the count sheet the office loads, so a filled-in sheet goes straight back to them. Do not change those two column headings.
