Logging a Ticket

Log a ticket when something is wrong, when you are stuck, or when you have a question or an idea. It goes straight to support and you get a ticket number immediately.

1 On your home page, click the Support Tickets icon at the top of the screen, next to Home and Refresh. Then click + Log a ticket.
2 Choose the kind of report: Something is wrong, I am stuck right now, Idea or improvement or Question.
3 If you are on a till login that the whole store shares, choose your name under Who is logging this? Replies go to the email address on your name.
4 Under In a few words, give the problem a short title.
5 Under What happened? What did you expect?, describe the steps you took and what you saw. Add any numbers that help: the invoice, the item code or the import sheet, for example KSI-00018.
6 Check Which screen?, which is usually filled in for you. Add a Screenshot if you have one, up to 4 MB.
7 Click Send to support. The ticket number appears on the screen. Replies come to you by email.

Checking Your Tickets

The Support Tickets screen lists everything your company has logged, newest first.

  • Use the search box to find a title, a ticket number or a person.
  • Click Open, Resolved or Closed to see only those tickets.
  • Tick Only mine to see only your own.
  • Click a row to see its detail.
